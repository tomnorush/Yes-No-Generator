Yes or No gives you a fast, honest answer to any yes-or-no question. Open the app and the answer is already on screen. Tap anywhere for another. No ads, no subscription, no account.

For when you can't decide and don't want to spend more than two seconds deciding.

INSTANT
• Your answer is on screen the moment the app opens. Zero taps.
• Tap anywhere for a new answer, with a light haptic tap.
• Need a third option? Switch to Yes / No / Maybe right on the main screen.

ASK OUT LOUD, OR FROM YOUR WRIST
• Say "Ask Yes or No" to Siri, or put it on the Action button for a one-press answer.
• Apple Watch app and complication: raise your wrist, tap, done.

SHARE THE VERDICT
• Type your question if you like ("Pizza tonight?") and copy or share the answer to your group chat in one tap.

REMEMBERS FOR YOU
• History keeps your recent answers and questions, so you can check what you decided last time.
• Swipe to delete one, or clear them all.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No data collected. No tracking, analytics, ads or third-party code.
• History stays on your device.
• Works fully offline and asks for no permissions.
• A tiny download, built natively for iPhone and Apple Watch.

HONEST
• Answers come from your device's secure random number generator.
• No pop-ups asking you to rate the app. Ever.
