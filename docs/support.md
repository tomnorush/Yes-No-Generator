# Yes or No: Help & Feedback

Yes or No gives you an instant answer to a yes-or-no question. Open the app and the answer is already there. Tap anywhere for another.

## Quick answers

- **How do I get a new answer?** Tap anywhere on the big answer. On Apple Watch, tap the screen, turn the Digital Crown, or double-tap your fingers (Series 9, Ultra 2 and later).
- **Can it say "maybe"?** Yes. Tap **Yes / No / Maybe** at the top of the screen. On Apple Watch, tap **Y/N** in the corner.
- **Can I ask with Siri?** Say "Ask Yes or No". You can also add "Get a Yes or No" to the Action button or a shortcut.
- **Where did my history go?** History is kept only on your device. Without Pro, the last 50 answers are kept. If Save History is off in Settings, new answers aren't saved.
- **Are the answers random?** Yes. They come from your device's secure random number generator. With Pro you can tilt the odds (for example 70% Yes); when you do, the main screen and anything you share always say so.
- **Does it work offline?** Everything except buying or restoring Pro works without a connection.

## Purchases

Yes or No is free, with no ads and no subscription. **Yes or No Pro** is an optional one-time purchase that adds color themes, custom odds and unlimited history.

- **Restore a purchase:** Settings → Purchases → **Restore Purchases**. It's always there, even before Pro is mentioned anywhere else.
- **Refunds** are handled by Apple: [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).
