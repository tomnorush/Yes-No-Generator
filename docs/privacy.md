# Privacy Policy

**Yes or No: Instant Answer** (iPhone and Apple Watch). Last updated: September 29, 2026.

## Summary

Yes or No collects no data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, tracking or third-party code in the app. What the app saves stays on your device, and the developer has no server that could receive it.

## What the app keeps on your device

- **Answer history**: each answer, when you got it, the question you typed (if any) and the odds, if you changed them from 50/50. You can turn history off, delete single answers or clear all of it at any time.
- **Settings**: appearance, color theme, haptics, answer type and odds.
- **Two local counters**: how many answers the app has shown you, and whether you've ever tapped for a new one. They are used only to hide the "Tap anywhere" hint and to wait until the app has helped you before mentioning Pro in Settings.
- **Apple Watch**: the watch app keeps only your answer type (Yes/No or Yes/No/Maybe). It has no history.

None of this is sent to the developer or anyone else.

## Backups

Like all app data on iPhone, the items above are included in your device backups (iCloud Backup, or a backup on your computer) if you use them. Apple handles those backups under your Apple Account; the developer can't access them.

## Deleting your data

- Swipe left on an answer in History to delete it, or use Clear All in History (or Clear History in Settings) to delete all of them.
- Deleting the app deletes everything it saved.

## Permissions

The app asks for no permissions: no location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, notifications or tracking. Copy puts text on your clipboard only when you tap it; the app never reads your clipboard. Share opens the standard iOS share sheet and sends only the answer text (and your question, if you typed one) to the place you pick.

## Network use

The core app works fully offline and makes no network requests of its own. The only internet use comes from Apple's App Store system (StoreKit), which iOS uses to show the price of Pro, process a purchase or restore, and keep your purchase record up to date, and from Help & Feedback, which opens the support page in your browser when you tap it.

## In-app purchase

Yes or No Pro is an optional one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple. The developer never receives your name, email address, payment details or Apple Account. The app checks whether Pro is unlocked using the signed purchase record that Apple keeps on your device. Apple gives developers aggregated sales reports that don't identify you.

## Siri, Shortcuts and the Action button

If you ask for an answer with Siri, the Shortcuts app or the Action button, Apple processes that request under Apple's own privacy policy. The answer itself is picked on your device and saved to your history like any other answer (unless history is off).

## Crash reports

The app contains no crash-reporting or analytics tools. If you have turned on Share With App Developers in iOS Settings (Privacy & Security, then Analytics & Improvements), Apple may share anonymous crash logs and usage statistics with the developer. You control that setting and can turn it off at any time.

## Children

The app is suitable for everyone and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes how it handles data, this policy will be updated first, both here and inside the app, with a new date at the top.
