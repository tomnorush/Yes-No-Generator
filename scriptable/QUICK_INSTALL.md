## Quick install on iPhone (Scriptable)

No Xcode, no App Store account, free. Takes about a minute.

1. Install [**Scriptable**](https://apps.apple.com/app/scriptable/id1405459188) from the App Store and open it once.
2. On your iPhone open [**Install Yes or No.js**](https://github.com/tomnorush/Yes-No-Generator/blob/main/scriptable/Install%20Yes%20or%20No.js)
   and tap **Copy raw file** (or **Raw**, then touch and hold the text → **Select All** → **Copy**).
3. In Scriptable tap **+**, paste, rename the script to `Install Yes or No`, and tap **Done**.
4. Tap **▶︎**. It downloads **Yes or No** into your scripts. Tap **Open Yes or No**.

**Use it:** the screen starts blank. Tap anywhere for an answer, and again for another. Yes / No / Maybe,
History, themes and odds are in **Settings**.

**Update:** run **Install Yes or No** again. Your history and settings are kept.
Siri, Action button, widgets and the share sheet: see the [full Scriptable guide](scriptable/README.md).
